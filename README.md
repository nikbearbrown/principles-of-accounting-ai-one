# Principles of Accounting (AI Plus One)

## About the book

This compact rough draft is for beginning business students and independent readers who know basic arithmetic but have not closed an accounting period. Follow the paperwork of a fictional repair shop from the first source record to financial statements. You will post a transaction, explain why cash and profit differ, and find a cutoff error that survives every arithmetic reconciliation. No spreadsheet, coding, or AI account is required.

## Start reading

Begin with [Trace a transaction twice](chapters/01-trace-a-transaction-twice.md) or use the [complete chapter guide](#chapter-guide). Read on GitHub or download the repository for offline Markdown. No separate formatted book edition is checked in.

## Chapter guide

### Part I · Turn evidence into balances

1. [Trace a transaction twice](chapters/01-trace-a-transaction-twice.md): Post debits and credits and state what a balanced trial balance cannot tell you.
2. [Recognize the event, not just the payment](chapters/02-recognize-the-event.md): Separate prepayments, receivables, and advances from what belongs in this month's income.
3. [Measure what remains](chapters/03-measure-what-remains.md): Reconcile parts by units and costs, and separate equipment depreciation from a cash payment.

### Part II · Close the month and challenge the result

4. [Read the statements together](chapters/04-read-the-statements-together.md): Reconcile the balance sheet, income statement and cash-flow statement through the same ledger.
5. [Audit the cutoff, not just the totals](chapters/05-audit-the-cutoff.md): Find and correct a balanced but premature revenue entry using independent acceptance evidence.

## How to teach or study it

Begin with the two sides of one entry before adding time. Work the inventory count and depreciation schedule before reconciling the statements. After the worked cases, close the [fictional repair shop](practice/may-close.md) from its source slips and submit a review explaining what the three numerical checks missed. Make a first attempt before looking at the [key](practice/may-close-key.md). The [queue blueprint](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/ai-plus-one-paper/blob/main/book-queue/editorial-blueprints/15-principles-of-accounting-ai-one.md) is a proposed teaching plan, not an adopted syllabus.
