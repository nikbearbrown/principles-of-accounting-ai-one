# Independent close: which job belongs in May?

Take the role of reviewer for a fictional repair business. Close May 31 using the [synthetic source slips](../data/may-close.json). Work unaided first. The shop records the case's service revenue at acceptance and assigns issued parts with FIFO. All openings not named in the data are zero. All dates and amounts are invented. Jobs paid in advance are separate from the $320 of completed cash and billed service. The six $20 parts were paid for; the four $25 parts were received on account and not paid in May. All seven issued parts were used on completed May work. The separate loan is not due in May. No tax, interest, owner contribution or draw occurs during May.

## What to submit

1. A dated or event-labeled journal and adjusted trial balance. Reconcile physical stock units to inventory purchases, usage and ending value.
2. An income statement, balance sheet and classified cash-flow statement. Show operating cash directly from movements and indirectly from reported income and operating-account changes. Include the opening-to-closing cash and equity bridges; do not plug a difference.
3. A one-paragraph review of the proposed batch: “release all three $100 advances as May service revenue.” Show what changes, what checks pass in spite of the error, which job-level record disagrees, and how you would correct the posted entry.
4. A transfer test: hold all posted amounts fixed but suppose job C's acceptance is unverified rather than June-dated. Explain what extra evidence you would require to recognize it in May. Do not manufacture a date.

## Evidence standard

An independent reader should be able to follow one cash event, one period-end adjustment, and the corrected advance from a source slip through the ledger to the statements. Allocate equal weight to posting/roll-forwards, accrual judgments, statement reconciliation, and the independently evidenced correction. A perfectly balanced trial balance cannot make up for a wrong cutoff.

Only after closing the month, compare against the [answer key](may-close-key.md) and [offline checks](../checks/README.md). If you use a model for critique, isolate one proposed entry and retain your original and revised work. Record the evidence you checked separately from the model. No external accounts or uploads are needed.
